No xylitol, chocolate, grapes/raisins, onion or garlic. Contains peanuts (PB flavor), egg and oats. Ask about your dog's allergies before ordering.

Storage & shelf life

  • No preservatives, so freshness is short.
  • Room temperature, airtight: about 5 to 7 days.
  • Refrigerated: about 2 weeks.
  • Frozen: up to about 2 months.
  • Each bag is labeled with flavor, ingredients, bake date and best-by date.
