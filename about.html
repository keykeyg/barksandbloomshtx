Our Story

A home kitchen, a love of dogs, and a love of flowers.

Barks and Blooms HTX is Nadia Godarzi's home-based business in Houston, Texas. It brings together two things that make a home feel happy: a dog waiting for a treat, and flowers on the table.

The dog treats started in Nadia's home kitchen with simple recipes and ingredients you can recognize. They are baked in small batches when an order comes in, so each bag is fresh.

The flowers come from the same hands-on approach. Fresh arrangements are put together for each order, and dried and preserved pieces use stems sourced from wholesale growers in Yunnan so they last for months.

Everything is made to order and delivered locally across Houston.
